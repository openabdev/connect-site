Releases

What each version brought, newest first. A version that has been submitted but not yet approved is listed here too.

OpenAB Connect for Mac Mac App Store ↗

1.6.1LatestMacSeptember 11, 2026
  • Session colours travel to your iPhone: when you share connections with OpenAB Remote, each session’s colour tag now goes along, so the phone’s session list and its swipe-to-switch overlay show the same colours as your tabs.
  • Requires OpenAB Remote 0.3.0; re-import connections on the phone after updating.
1.6.0MacSeptember 10, 2026
  • Redesigned the “Remove connection” dialog for Kubernetes deployments: one Delete action plus two optional checkboxes — keep the pod running (orphan it), and keep its volume for reuse by a new connection.
  • A retained volume is marked shared, so it appears in the Kubernetes management panel where you can reuse or delete it later — no more orphaned volumes you can’t reach.
1.5.2MacSeptember 9, 2026
  • Switching panes with ⌘[ / ⌘] now moves keyboard focus straight to the terminal, so you can start typing immediately — no mouse click needed.
1.5.1MacSeptember 8, 2026
  • Maintenance update: refreshed Mac App Store screenshots. No functional changes.
1.5.0MacSeptember 7, 2026
  • Persistent workspaces: mount an existing PVC as a session's /workspace, or keep it ephemeral. A volume you point the app at is borrowed, not owned — removing the connection never deletes it, so data can outlive any one session and be reused by the next.
  • New Kubernetes management panel (Kubernetes menu): create, list and delete shared PVCs and Secrets that live independently of any connection.
  • Expose a key from a shared Secret as an agent environment variable, without typing the value into the app.
  • Copy an existing PVC’s contents into a new one, right from the app — no CSI required.
  • Volumes already in use by a running pod are marked and can’t be mounted twice by mistake.
1.4.0MacSeptember 3, 2026
  • New “Copy files” button in the sidebar: pick a source and one or more destinations, and copy files or folders between your Mac and session hosts — and between hosts — scp-style over your existing kubectl access. Nothing new to install on the host.
  • Drag and drop a file or folder straight into the sheet, or click to choose.
  • Host-to-host copies stream through your Mac, so one source can fan out to several hosts in a single run.
  • Right-click a Kubernetes connection and choose “Copy files…” to start with that host as the source.
1.3.1MacSeptember 2, 2026
  • New sessions on the Nightly channel now always pull the latest image. A nightly tag moves with every build, so a cached copy on your cluster meant a new session could silently run an older runtime.
  • The Pre-beta channel is labelled “testing” rather than “stable” — the name promised a support level that does not exist yet.
1.3.0MacSeptember 2, 2026
  • A dark, unified window chrome with Ghostty-style session tabs. Press Command-T or use the trailing + to open a new session on the selected connection.
  • Redesigned connection cards group each host and show Kubernetes or ECS platform marks at a glance.
  • The tab context menu can detach and close, rename, or colour a tab; the inactive pane now dims when a split is focused.
  • More reliable recovery from dropped attaches, Devin TUI support, and an ephemeral-workspace disclosure that fades after eight seconds.
1.2.0MacAugust 31, 2026
  • Copy on select: releasing a text selection in the terminal copies it to the clipboard automatically, iTerm-style.
  • Dictation language: choose English or Traditional Chinese (台灣) for Press to Speak.
  • Dictation is now fully on-device — the Groq Cloud option was removed and audio never leaves your Mac.
  • Dictation setup problems now explain themselves in a dialog with the exact System Settings switch to flip.
1.1.0MacAugust 30, 2026
  • Links in terminal output are clickable: hold Command to highlight a URL, Command-click to open it in your browser. Only http/https links open; everything else is refused for safety.
  • New session hosts let you choose how long a detached session is kept — 1 to 168 hours, default 72. Every session ends after one week at most.
  • Fixed the sidebar losing your selection every few seconds while it refreshed session state in the background.
1.0.1MacAugust 28, 2026

Updates the OpenAB Connect app icon.

1.0MacAugust 27, 2026

First release on the Mac App Store. Agents from every major vendor in one client, each in its own sandboxed container on your Kubernetes cluster or AWS ECS Fargate — sessions outlive the app, nothing is exposed to the network, and your iPhone works as a push-to-talk remote.

OpenAB Remote for iPhone App Store ↗

0.3.1In reviewiPhoneSubmitted September 11, 2026
  • More recognition languages: Cantonese (Hong Kong), Mandarin (China), Japanese and Korean join Taiwanese Mandarin and English. Each downloads its on-device model the first time you choose it.
  • The recognition language is now picked from a menu in Settings instead of a crowded row.
0.3.0LatestiPhoneSeptember 11, 2026
  • Swipe between sessions: swipe in from the left or right edge of the terminal to move to the previous or next session; the order wraps around at the ends like a TV remote. A brief overlay names the connection and session (and its position, e.g. 2 / 5), and the keyboard stays as you left it.
  • Session colours: colour tags set on your Mac travel with shared connections and appear in the session list and the switch overlay (import connections again from OpenAB Connect 1.6.1).
  • Settings: Auto-Enter, microphone mode (hold to talk, or tap to start and stop) and language now live in Settings behind the gear button; Demo mode and the diagnostic log moved under Advanced.
  • Fixes: the Talk button no longer goes dead while a session is open in the Sessions tab, and a pairing that fails to connect now says so instead of waiting on “Confirm on your Mac”.
0.2.3iPhoneSeptember 8, 2026
  • Maintenance update: refreshed App Store screenshots and minor housekeeping. No functional changes.
0.2.2iPhoneSeptember 7, 2026
  • New Settings screen — tap the gear in the top bar.
  • Choose your language: Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, English, Japanese, or Korean.
  • Press Enter after inserting — automatically send a newline after dictated or typed text reaches your Mac (on by default).
  • Microphone mode — hold to talk, or tap once to start and tap again to stop.
  • Fixed terminal sessions rendering at the wrong width on first open, and removed stray color-query characters when attaching some sessions.
0.2.1iPhoneSeptember 2, 2026
  • Importing connections from your Mac now mirrors the Mac exactly — connections you removed there no longer linger here.
  • Fixed stray characters that could appear when attaching to a session another device had already opened.
0.2.0iPhoneSeptember 2, 2026

First release on the App Store. Pair with your Mac running OpenAB Connect and attach to any running session from your iPhone — the transcript replays and you can type or dictate. Press to Speak turns your voice into text on-device, and an offline demo lets you try every screen without pairing.
